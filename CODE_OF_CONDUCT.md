# Code of conduct

KinematiK is an engineering tool used by students, teams and engineers who disagree about physics for a living. Disagreement is welcome. This document sets out how it is done here.

## What is expected

- Argue with the engineering, not the person. Name the module, the equation or the number you think is wrong, and say why.
- Assume good faith. Most people opening an issue are on a Formula Student team with a deadline.
- Be specific and be brief. A reproducible case is worth more than a long opinion.
- Credit other people's work, data and results.
- Accept that a technical objection can be answered with evidence, and that sometimes the answer is that you were right.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, university, team or level of experience.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including team data from a shared workspace, without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after it has been answered, or dismissing a contribution without engaging with its content.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews in this repository, the hosted app's shared workspaces, and any space where someone is representing KinematiK.

## Reporting

Email kinematik.kng1q@8shield.net with "KinematiK conduct" in the subject. Include links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential; you will get an acknowledgement within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask for a correction, edit or remove the content, or temporarily or permanently block the person from the repository and the hosted app. Decisions are explained to the person reported unless that would put the reporter at risk.
