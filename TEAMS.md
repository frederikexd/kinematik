# Teams Using KinematiK

KinematiK is currently being tested by members from 42 FSAE teams across six continents.

These teams use KinematiK for prevalidating potential design choices to reduce the garbage in, garbage out phenomenon before validating them in established softwares such as ADAMS, Solidworks and ANSYS as part of their active build cycles.

If your team wants access, the repo is open and the app is live.
For workspace setup, open an issue or contact the maintainer.
