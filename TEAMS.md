# Teams Using KinematiK

KinematiK is currently being tested by members from 42 FSAE teams across six continents.

These teams use KinematiK for documenting and prevalidating potential design choices by observing how they could affect another subsystem in real time, effectively reducing the garbage in, garbage out phenomenon before validating them in established softwares such as ADAMS, Solidworks and ANSYS as part of their active build cycles.

If your team wants access, the repo is open and the app is live.
For workspace setup, open an issue or contact the maintainer.
